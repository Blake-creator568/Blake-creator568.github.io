About Orbis Vox

Orbis Vox was founded with a simple but ambitious intention: to create journalism that informs with clarity, challenges with integrity, and speaks with purpose. The name itself means “Voice of the World,” and that is precisely the standard it strives to uphold.

Established by Blake at just sixteen years old, Orbis Vox was built from the ground up as an independent platform driven by curiosity, critical thinking, and a commitment to understanding the deeper layers of global and local issues. Rather than chasing noise or sensationalism, the publication focuses on context — the histories, systems, and human factors that shape the stories we see every day.

Blake's approach to journalism is rooted in observation and analysis. Whether exploring complex social issues, local priorities, or real-life pressures facing young people today, Orbis Vox seeks to examine not only what happened, but why it happened — and what it means moving forward.

As it continues to grow, the vision remains clear: to expand Orbis Vox into a globally recognised, printed news publication that values substance over spectacle and truth over trend.
